Quick start for Voice boards, chats, and speaking.
  1. Sidebar — bottom icons for Voice, History, Topics, Settings, and Help. On phone, the record_voice_over button opens the drawer.
  2. Top bar — always visible; tap empty space on the bar (or swipe up/down) to toggle between normal and slim (50% height). Chat chips and the menu button stay full width.
  3. Topic chats — each open topic is its own chat (1–5, no duplicates). Tap an inactive chip to switch; tap the active chip for the topics menu.
  4. Sounds — grid_view buttons play their audio.
  5. Text box — more_horiz Actions for clear, pin, replay, tags, and history; volume_up speaker speaks (or press Enter).
