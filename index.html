lightbulb Quick start
  1. Menu — record_voice_over logo opens the drawer (Voice, History, Topics). Bottom menu Menu has Appearance, Settings, and Help.
  2. Top bar — pull the expand_more chevron or swipe down to show chats; close collapses it.
  3. Topic chats — each open topic is its own chat (1–5, no duplicates). Tap an inactive chip to switch; tap the active chip for the topics menu.
  4. Sounds — grid_view buttons play their audio.
  5. Text box — more_horiz Actions for clear, pin, replay, tags, and history; volume_up speaker speaks (or press Enter).
