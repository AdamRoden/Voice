lightbulb Quick start
  1. Logo — top-left opens the menu (Settings, History, Topics). Use the chevron at the top-right of the menu to collapse it.
  2. Top bar (left → right) — new message clears the box; 1 / 2 / 3 switch drafts; pin assigns your text to a sound button.
  3. Board — tap a button to speak; the small + on a button inserts its text into the message.
  4. Composer (left → right) — + inserts a tag; topic switches boards; type in the box; blue speaker speaks (or press Enter).
  5. Message — words under the box; tap × to remove one; use Replay when available.
  6. Recent — tap to insert a phrase; double-tap to speak it.
  7. Sidebar — Settings (voice & look), History, Topics; Export / Import under Topics; Help at the bottom.
