Word class lexicon (top ~10k)
=============================

File: word-class-10k.json.gz

Payload:
  words[]   — frequency-ordered vocab (mid-word prefix scan)
  classes{} — POS lists (also frequency-ordered within each class)

Frequency order from:
  google-10000-english-usa-no-swears.txt
  first20hours/google-10000-english
  https://github.com/first20hours/google-10000-english

Part-of-speech tags primarily from:
  Moby Part-of-Speech II (Grady Ward / Moby Project)
  Public domain
  https://github.com/elitejake/Moby-Project

Closed-class labels (determiners, pronouns, modals, auxiliaries,
prepositions, conjunctions, wh-words, negation) are curated for AAC
keyboard use and take precedence over Moby multi-tags.

Words not found in Moby (~23%) are assigned via light morphology
heuristics, defaulting to noun.

Rebuild:
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/build-word-class-10k.ps1
