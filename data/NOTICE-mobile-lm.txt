Mobile language model data
==========================

File: mobile-lm.json.gz

Derived from "Forum, 64k, 4-gram, large" in:
  Dataset 3: Forum only language models
  Keith Vertanen and Per Ola Kristensson (2019)
  https://digitalcommons.mtu.edu/mobiletext/3/

Supporting paper:
  Mining, Analyzing, and Modeling Text Written on Mobile Devices
  https://digitalcommons.mtu.edu/michigantech-p/934/

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The compact JSON retains top-K next-word continuations (and unigram
log-probabilities) for browser use; it is not a full ARPA model.

Export settings (tools/convert-arpa-mobile-lm.ps1):
  source: lm_forum_64k_4gram_large.arpa.gz
  topK: bi=48, tri=32, four=20
  max contexts: tri=220000, four=160000
